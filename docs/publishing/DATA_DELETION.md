## If you are a staff user

### Cloud mode (shared online database)

**Delete it yourself, inside the App:**

1. Sign in and open **Settings**.
2. Find the **Delete my account** card.
3. Type **DELETE** in the box and tap **Delete my account**.
4. Your sign-in account and your user profile (name, email, role) are deleted at once, and you are signed out.

If you are the **only active admin**, the App will not let you delete your account. Make another user an admin first, so the business is never locked out.

**An admin can delete another user:** **Settings → Users & roles → Edit** (on that user) → **Delete user** → confirm. This removes that person’s login for good. To only stop someone signing in, untick **Active (can sign in)** instead.

**Or ask us:** use the email button above if you can no longer sign in.

**What stays:** business records you created (for example leads, invoices or notes) belong to the business and stay, as tax law requires. The “created by” link to you is cleared. Your name may still appear where staff typed it, until the business edits those records.

### Device mode (data only on one device)

Everything is stored in the web browser on that device. To delete it all, an admin can download a backup first if needed, then clear the site’s data:

- **Android (Chrome):** Chrome → ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → All sites → find the App’s website → Delete data / Clear & reset.
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → find the website → Delete. If you added it to the Home Screen, also remove the icon.
- **Windows / Mac (Chrome or Edge):** open the website → click the icon left of the address → Site settings → Delete data.

Uninstalling the app icon alone may **not** delete the data, because the browser keeps it. Clear the site data as shown above.

To remove only your own user from that device, use **Settings → Delete my account** (type DELETE). The business records on the device stay. An admin can also remove another user (**Settings → Users & roles → Edit → Delete user**) or just switch them off (untick **Active**).

## If you are a customer, lead, vendor or employee of a solar business

The solar business decides what it records about you. You can:

1. Ask the business directly, or
2. Email us using the button above. Name the business. We will pass the request to it, or act on it if we run the App for that business.

How the business deletes your record:

- An **admin or manager** opens the record and taps **Delete**.
- The App will not delete a record that other records still use (for example, a customer who has a quotation, project or invoice). The linked records must be deleted or changed first.
- **Invoices can never be deleted**, only cancelled. Payments linked to an invoice stay with it.
- In cloud mode, deleting a record also deletes its uploaded file from storage. If that step fails (for example the connection drops), the App says so, and the admin can delete the file in the Supabase dashboard: **Storage → files → find the file → Delete**.
- Enquiry-form submissions become normal leads, and are deleted the same way.

## What we cannot delete straight away

Some records must be kept by law, even after you ask:

| Record | Why it is kept | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Tax invoices, receipts, payments, purchase orders, expenses and the customer and vendor details printed on them (name, address, GSTIN, state) | Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 requires books of account and records to be kept | Until 72 months from the due date of the annual return for that year, and longer if an appeal, investigation or proceeding is pending |
| Books of account of a company | Companies Act, 2013 | Eight financial years |
| Employee salary and attendance records | Labour and income-tax laws | As those laws require |
| Records needed for a legal claim or dispute | To establish or defend legal claims | Until the matter ends |

When a record must be kept, we and the business will delete everything else (for example phone number, email, photos, documents, notes), stop using the kept record for anything except the legal purpose, and delete it when the period ends.
