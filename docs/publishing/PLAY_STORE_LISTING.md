### Data safety

These answers describe a **cloud mode** build, which is what a Play listing will normally wrap. If your listed website runs in device mode, no data leaves the device and you may answer “No data collected”, but cloud mode is the realistic case.

Play counts data as **collected** when it leaves the device, even if it goes to a service provider working for you. It counts data as **shared** only when it goes to a third party that is not your service provider. Supabase, the web host and the email-sending service act for the Business, so nothing is “shared”.
