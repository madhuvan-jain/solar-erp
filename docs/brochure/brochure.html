01Overview
What it is

One app for yourSolar business

SuryaSetu is business software built for Indian solar installers and EPC companies. Sales, site surveys, quotations, projects, installation, stock, purchase, GST billing, subsidy, loans, service and your team — all connected, so a lead becomes a project, an invoice, a payment and an AMC without re-typing anything.

Why choose it

  1. 01Everything in one place

    12 business apps plus dashboard, reports, alerts and settings. No more spreadsheets, WhatsApp notes and separate billing software.

  2. 02Made for Indian solar

    PM Surya Ghar subsidy tracking, net-metering milestones, kW-based projects, solar loans with EMI, amounts in lakh / crore words.

  3. 03GST done right

    CGST + SGST or IGST from the place of supply, HSN/SAC summary, round-off and a fresh invoice series every financial year (INV/26-27/0001).

  4. 04Get paid faster

    Every unpaid invoice prints a UPI QR code for the exact balance. Share documents on WhatsApp; see receivables ageing at a glance.

  5. 05Stock you can trust

    Received purchase orders add stock, dispatched challans take it out — once. Stock can never go negative and low stock raises an alert.

  6. 06See India at a glance

    A live India map shades states by sales or customers and plots every customer as a dot. Every list can be shown on the map too.

  7. 07Works where your team works

    Same app on phone, tablet and desktop. Install it from the browser — no app store needed. Starts even without internet.

  8. 08Safe for teams

    Five roles, only admin and manager can delete, issued invoices are cancelled — never deleted, and two people editing the same record get a warning.

Who it is for

Rooftop installers

Residential 2–10 kW systems with subsidy and bank loans.

C&I EPC companies

Commercial and industrial plants, multi-stage projects, POs.

Dealers & distributors

Panels, inverters and BOS supply with GST invoices and stock.

O&M & service teams

Tickets, cleaning visits and AMC contracts with renewals.

GST billing &Payments

Tax invoices your CA will like, and a QR code your customer can pay in seconds.

  1. 01Financial-year numbering

    INV/26-27/0001 restarts every April. Receipts, quotations, POs and challans follow the same rule.

  4. 04Original / Duplicate / Triplicate

  5. 05Payments & receipts

    Cash, UPI, bank transfer, cheque, card, loan disbursal or subsidy; advances; receipt PDF. Overpayment is blocked.

  6. 06Status from payments

    Unpaid, Partially Paid or Paid is worked out from what was received — never typed by hand.

  7. 07Audit-safe

    Issued invoices cannot be deleted — cancel them instead. Due date from your payment terms.

  8. 08Expenses & statements
