1. Free trial

Every new business gets 15 days free, with every feature and any number of users. No card or payment details are asked for. There is one free trial per email address and per mobile number, also after an account is deleted and made again.

3. How to pay

  • The business’s admin pays on our website: Settings → Subscription, then a plan.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay: UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets. We never see or store card or bank details.
  • Paying before the current period ends adds the new plan after it, so no days are lost.
  • Plans do not renew automatically. Nothing is charged unless the admin pays.

4. Delivery

The service is delivered online. A plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute. Nothing is shipped.

5. When a trial or plan ends

Changes are paused, but the business’s data stays available: everyone can still view it, print it and export it. Buying a plan opens it again at once. We keep the data of a business whose plan has ended for at least 12 months, and we email its admin at least 30 days before deleting it.

6. Cancellation

You can stop at any time: simply do not buy another plan. There is nothing to cancel, because plans do not renew by themselves. To remove your business and its data completely, every user deletes their account (Settings → My account → Delete my account); when the last person does so, the business and all its records are deleted at once, and its uploaded files (photos, documents) within 15 days. See Delete account.
