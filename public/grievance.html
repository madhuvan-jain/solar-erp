This officer is named under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”). This person can also answer questions about how personal data is processed.

What you can complain about

  • How your personal data is collected, used, corrected or deleted.
  • A request to see, correct or erase your data that was not handled.
  • A suspected security problem or data breach.
  • Content in the App that you believe is unlawful.
  • Problems with your account or with these policies.

If you are a customer of a solar business that uses the App, that business is responsible for your data. You can complain to it directly. You can also write to us and we will pass it on or help.

How to complain

Email [support email] with the subject Grievance, or write to the address above. Please include:

  1. Your name and how to contact you.
  2. The name of the solar business (if you are its customer, lead or employee).
  3. What happened, with dates, and any screenshots or record numbers (for example an invoice number like INV/26-27/0001).
  4. What you would like us to do.

Please do not send full Aadhaar numbers, bank passwords or OTPs.

Timelines

StepTime
AcknowledgementWithin 24 hours
Resolution, or a reasoned replyWithin 15 days of receiving the complaint

These times meet the limits in the IT Rules, 2011 (one month) and the Intermediary Rules, 2021 (acknowledge in 24 hours, resolve in 15 days). The DPDP Act also lets rules set a time limit for answering grievances; we aim to reply well within it.

If you are not satisfied

  • Personal data complaints: after using this process, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act. The Act expects you to use the grievance process first.
  • Other complaints about content under the Intermediary Rules: where those rules apply, you may appeal to a Grievance Appellate Committee set up by the Government of India, within 30 days of our decision.
  • You may also go to the courts at [Jurisdiction city], as stated in our Terms of Service.
